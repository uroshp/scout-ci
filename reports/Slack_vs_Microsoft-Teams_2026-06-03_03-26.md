# 🟣 Competitive Intelligence Brief: Slack vs. Microsoft Teams
---

## Executive Summary

**1. The EU unbundling settlement is a structural opportunity — and it applies worldwide.**


The European Union accepted commitments from Microsoft to unbundle its Teams workplace communication platform from its popular productivity apps, exempting Microsoft from a potentially hefty antitrust fine after the company was accused of breaching competition rules with the "abusive" bundling of Teams and Office products.
 
Microsoft has promised that for the next seven years, it will provide Microsoft 365 and Office 365 without Teams at a lower price and will let customers choose whether they want to pay more to add the collaboration app.
 Critically, 
Microsoft extended the unbundling requirements globally to ensure consistency for customers worldwide.
 
As of November 1, 2025, Microsoft's modular licensing model allows customers to mix and match plans, with or without Teams — a shift that is partly thanks to EU commitments but affects organizations worldwide.


**So what:** Every enterprise renewal conversation from now through 2032 is a competitive door. Slack reps should audit Microsoft EA renewals happening post-November 2025 and make the cost of adding Teams back explicit. The first time a procurement department has to actively choose Teams is the first time they can choose Slack instead.

---

**2. Copilot's true total cost is a procurement grenade — and it's hidden in plain sight.**


Microsoft 365 Copilot is sold as an add-on license, not a standalone product. The list price for the enterprise add-on is \$30 per user per month on an annual commitment.
 
This requires an existing, qualifying Microsoft 365 license such as Business Standard, Business Premium, E3, or E5.
 
For most large enterprises, the qualifying base is M365 E3, currently \$36/user/month — but Microsoft announced in December 2025 that E3 increases to \$39 effective July 1, 2026, and E5 moves from \$57 to \$60.
 That puts a fully loaded Copilot user at \$69/month starting July 2026. 
At \$30 per user per month, Copilot often increases the per-user Microsoft 365 cost by 40–60% depending on the base license tier.


Meanwhile, 
EA renewals after November 1, 2025 face dual pressure: the Teams rebundling change is occurring in parallel with the removal of Enterprise Agreement volume discounts, both taking effect on the same date.


**So what:** In any deal where a prospect is evaluating Copilot, model the all-in per-user cost of M365 + Copilot (\$69/month from July 2026) versus Slack's paid plans. Slack AI — channel summaries, enterprise search, huddle notes, Agentforce — is included with paid Slack plans, with no \$30 add-on required. The gap is decisive for teams running mixed-cloud stacks.

---

**3. Slack's Agentforce + CRM integration is currently unmatched for Salesforce-centric enterprises — and is accelerating.**


Agentforce, the first digital labor platform for enterprises, is now available in Slack, allowing businesses to deploy trusted, autonomous AI agents across departments including sales, service, marketing, and commerce — so every employee can engage with Agentforce directly in their flow of work.
 
Agentforce Sales, IT and HR Service, and Tableau Next bring AI agents and CRM data into the flow of work, surfacing insights and driving faster action.
 
AI-enabled apps built for Slack have already grown 690% year over year.



New RTS API and MCP server capabilities enable developers and partners — like OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Perplexity, Writer, Dropbox, Notion, Cognition Labs, Vercel, and Cursor — to build and deploy agents directly in Slack, powered by real-time conversational context.


**So what:** In any account running Salesforce CRM, Slack's pitch is not "better chat." It is "your CRM is now conversational." Lead with Agentforce use cases — pipeline updates, account briefings, IT incident response — rather than messaging features. This is the single strongest displacement play Slack has right now.

---

**4. The scale gap between Teams and Slack is real and should shape how you pitch, not whether you pitch.**


Microsoft Teams reached 320 million monthly active users in early 2024.
 
The platform's integration with Microsoft 365 applications has contributed to its popularity, with 93% of Fortune 100 companies relying on it for their operations.
 
Microsoft has been named a Leader in the 2025 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for UCaaS worldwide, marking its seventh consecutive year with this recognition, positioned highest for ability to execute and furthest for completeness of vision.


**So what:** Slack cannot and should not compete on ubiquity. The winning frame is precision — Slack is the right tool for the teams inside Microsoft shops that run Salesforce, GitHub, and Jira, not SharePoint and Outlook. Avoid broad displacement pitches; focus on winning the high-value teams where Slack's ecosystem advantage is concrete.

## Snapshot: Microsoft Teams

**What it is:** Microsoft Teams is an enterprise collaboration platform combining persistent messaging, video meetings, file sharing (via SharePoint/OneDrive), telephony (Teams Phone), and AI assistance (Microsoft 365 Copilot). It is the centerpiece of Microsoft's Productivity and Business Processes segment.

**Scale:** 
Microsoft Teams reached 320 million monthly active users in early 2024.
 
Over 8 million US companies use Microsoft Teams for work.


**Customer mix:** 
The platform supports a diverse range of company sizes: 21% small businesses, 47% medium-sized businesses, and 32% large enterprises.


**Industry concentration:** 
The Information Technology sector has the maximum adoption share at 29.6%, followed by Computer Software (11.8%), Healthcare (9.6%), and Education Management (8.3%).


**Analyst standing:** 
Microsoft has been named a Leader in the 2025 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Unified Communications as a Service worldwide, marking its seventh consecutive year, positioned highest for ability to execute — recognition Microsoft cites for innovations including the launch of agents in meetings and a redesigned chat and channels experience.


**Market position in video conferencing:** 
Teams holds a 32.29% share of the global video conferencing market.


**Teams Phone:** 
There are about 80 million Teams Phone users, of which over 20 million use PSTN calling.
 This positions Teams as a genuine unified communications replacement for legacy phone systems — a capability Slack does not directly offer.

**Uptime SLA:** Microsoft commits to 99.9% uptime for Teams as part of the Microsoft 365 SLA. Slack's Enterprise Grid SLA guarantees 99.99% uptime — a notably higher contractual target.

## Recent Strategic Moves

**1. EU Antitrust Settlement — September 12, 2025**


The EU accepted commitments from Microsoft to unbundle Teams from its popular productivity apps, exempting Microsoft from a potentially hefty antitrust fine after the company was accused of breaching competition rules with the "abusive" bundling of Teams and Office products.
 
Microsoft's commitments include offering versions of Office 365 and Microsoft 365 without Teams at an appreciably lower price, giving customers recurrent opportunities to switch to suites without Teams, ensuring interoperability for key functionalities between competing tools and Microsoft products, and allowing users to export their Teams messaging data to competing solutions.
 
Microsoft must comply with these commitments for seven years, with interoperability and data portability obligations extending to ten years.


*Signal:* Teams lost its most powerful distribution mechanism — default installation on every M365 seat. Every new enterprise deal now requires a deliberate procurement decision on Teams.

---

**2. Global Licensing Restructuring — November 1, 2025**


As of November 1, 2025, Microsoft's modular licensing model allows customers to mix and match plans, with or without Teams — a shift partly driven by EU commitments but affecting organizations worldwide.
 
For M365 E3 or higher, Teams adds collaboration at a fixed price delta of \$8.55/user/month through at least November 2032.



Critically, this change is occurring in parallel with the removal of Enterprise Agreement volume discounts, also taking effect November 1, 2025.
 Enterprise customers renewing after this date face double complexity: they must explicitly decide on Teams bundling while simultaneously losing established volume discount structures.

*Signal:* Procurement departments now must make an explicit Teams decision at every EA renewal. That window is Slack's opening.

---

**3. Copilot Expansion Into Teams — Throughout 2025**


Copilot Chat is available at no additional cost to all Microsoft Entra ID users with an eligible Microsoft 365 subscription.
 
Full Microsoft 365 Copilot — the deep integration across all apps — is sold as an add-on license at \$30 per user per month on an annual commitment.


*Signal:* Microsoft is making Copilot ambient — a free-tier Copilot Chat presence in every Teams interface, with full Copilot as the \$30/user upsell. This is a deliberate adoption funnel. Know the difference when prospects cite "Copilot is included."

---

**4. Teams AI Interpreter — 2025**


As of 2025, Teams offers an AI Interpreter mode that allows meeting participants to speak in their own language. AI translates and delivers content using neural speech synthesis to simulate the original speaker's voice. The initial rollout supports nine languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Chinese.
 
The feature requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot license, with 20 hours of interpretation included per user per month.


*Signal:* This is a genuine differentiator for multinational enterprises with cross-language meeting needs. Slack has no equivalent. Flag this in any deal involving large multilingual workforces.

## Competitive Battlecard

### ✅ WHERE SLACK WINS

**Against: Salesforce-centric enterprises; developer-heavy organizations; companies prioritizing cross-company collaboration; mixed-cloud-stack mid-market.**

**1. Cross-company collaboration (Slack Connect)**


Slack Connect is used by more than 100,000 organizations, including 77 of the Fortune 100 companies.
 
Each Slack Connect channel can host up to 250 organizations, including your own.


> **Soundbite:** *"With Slack Connect, your customers and vendors work in the same channel you do — no email, no friction. Teams guest access requires multi-step setup through Entra B2B and still breaks features. If your business lives on external collaboration, that gap costs you daily."*

**2. App ecosystem and developer experience**


The Slack App Directory now lists more than 2,600 apps and integrations in the Slack Marketplace.
 
Top integrations include Salesforce, Asana, Zoom, HubSpot, GitHub, and Notion, with two-way data sync for the major ones.


> **Soundbite:** *"If your team builds on GitHub, Jira, and Salesforce — not Word, SharePoint, and Outlook — Slack's integrations are native and real-time. Teams integrations are an afterthought to the M365 ecosystem."*

**3. Salesforce/CRM AI (Agentforce in Slack)**


Intelligent AI agents and CRM data from Salesforce are now embedded directly into Slack conversations, starting with Sales, IT, HR Service, and Tableau. Agentforce Sales in Slack brings people, data, and workflows together in one conversational workspace, enabling sales teams to collaborate, surface AI-driven insights, and take action directly alongside live CRM customer data.



Salesforce has a Teams integration, and it works. But it's not where the R&D investment is going. The Slackbot reimagining, the Agentforce-native experiences, and enterprise search connectors are all Slack-first. Teams gets functional parity on the basics, but the agentic future Salesforce is building is a Slack story.


> **Soundbite:** *"Copilot is smart inside Word and Outlook. Agentforce in Slack is smart about your customers — pulling live Salesforce data, suggesting next steps, and updating records without your reps ever leaving the conversation."*

### ⚔️ WHERE IT IS A FIGHT

**Contested: Mid-market enterprises running mixed stacks; companies evaluating Copilot vs. Slack AI; organizations in active EA renewals.**

**1. AI productivity features**

Both platforms have made heavy AI investments. The fight tips toward Teams when the buyer is fully invested in M365 and willing to pay the Copilot add-on; it tips toward Slack when the buyer runs Salesforce and uses a mixed-cloud stack. The decisive factor is toolstack, not features.

**2. Pricing (post-unbundling)**


For M365 E3 or higher, Teams adds collaboration at a fixed price delta of \$8.55/user/month through at least November 2032.
 The pricing fight is genuinely close for the base product. It decisively tilts Slack's way when the prospect adds M365 Copilot at \$30/user/month and the buyer uses non-Microsoft tools.

**Tip toward Slack when:** The account is in an active EA renewal. 
The Teams rebundling change is occurring in parallel with the removal of EA volume discounts, both taking effect November 1, 2025 — meaning EA renewals after that date face dual pressure: lost volume discounts and unclear Teams bundle pricing.

Negative sentiment is pervasive across review platforms. Users consistently describe Teams as sluggish, RAM-intensive, and prone to notification failures. ## Objection Handling

---

**Objection 1: "We already pay for Microsoft 365. Teams is basically free — why would we pay for Slack?"**

**Response:**


As of November 1, 2025, Microsoft's modular licensing model requires Teams to be a separate, explicit purchase — no longer bundled automatically. This change is worldwide.
 
For M365 E3 or higher, Teams adds collaboration at a fixed price delta of \$8.55/user/month.


So the real question is: does Teams at \$8.55/user — or significantly more once you factor in Copilot add-ons and the EA restructuring — deliver more value than Slack for your specific teams?

For teams running Salesforce, GitHub, Jira, or Asana, the answer is often no. Slack's AI (channel summaries, enterprise search, Agentforce) is included in paid plans, with no \$30/user/month add-on required.

*Ask the prospect:* "How many of your teams actually live in SharePoint and Outlook vs. Salesforce, GitHub, or Figma?" The answer usually reveals the M365 discount argument applies to some teams and not others.

---

**Objection 2: "Microsoft is investing heavily in Copilot AI. Isn't Slack's AI advantage negated?"**

**Response:**

Copilot in Teams is real and improving. But it is a Microsoft-first AI. 
Copilot performs well within the Microsoft ecosystem. Connecting third-party tools like Confluence, Salesforce, or ServiceNow requires custom connector development — schema definition, Entra ID registration, and ingestion code.


Slack's AI is model-agnostic and ecosystem-open. 
New RTS API and MCP server capabilities enable developers and partners — like OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Perplexity, Writer, Dropbox, Notion, Cognition Labs, Vercel, and Cursor — to build and deploy agents directly in Slack, powered by real-time conversational context.


The honest comparison: if your work happens inside M365 apps, Copilot's value is high. If your work spans Salesforce, GitHub, and Notion — which describes most tech companies — Slack's AI has more context and more reach.

---

**Objection 3: "Our IT team prefers keeping everything in one Microsoft ecosystem for security and compliance."**

**Response:**

This is the legitimate version of the lock-in argument and deserves a real answer, not dismissal.

The interoperability commitment changes the technical moat. 
Under the EU settlement, Microsoft must ensure interoperability for key functionalities between tools that compete with Teams and Microsoft products, and allow users to export their Teams messaging data for use in competing solutions.


Slack is also enterprise-grade. Slack's Business+ and Enterprise+ plans provide SAML-based SSO, data loss prevention, corporate export capabilities for compliance and e-discovery, and dedicated security key enforcement. Slack runs alongside M365 in thousands of enterprises today.

The practical response: IT consolidation is a legitimate concern when IT owns the decision. The counter is to find the teams — engineering, sales, product — who are Slack's actual champions, and build bottom-up momentum that forces a more nuanced conversation about fit vs. consolidation.
