# Competitive Intelligence Brief: Anthropic vs. OpenAI

## Executive Summary

**1. Anthropic has overtaken OpenAI in both run-rate revenue and private valuation — this is the single most important commercial narrative shift of 2026.**

Anthropic reported a $47 billion revenue run rate in late May 2026, up from a $30 billion run rate earlier this year and $10 billion in annual revenue last year. ([Anthropic tops OpenAI as most valuable AI startup, nears...](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-open-ai-startup-value.html)) These are company-stated, unaudited run-rates — not filed revenue — disclosed in conjunction with fundraising announcements. OpenAI's run rate stood at approximately $33 billion as of May 2026 (Sacra estimate). ([Anthropic revenue, valuation & funding | Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/anthropic/)) On quarterly actuals, Anthropic generated $4.8 billion in revenue during Q1 2026 and is on track to generate $10.9 billion in Q2 — a figure that would top its sales for all of last year — and if hit, would mark its first profitable quarter. ([Anthropic set to hit $10.9 billion in revenue during seco...](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-revenue-explosive-growth-ipo-profitable-quarter.html)) Meanwhile, Anthropic closed a $65 billion Series H at a $965 billion valuation, putting it above rival OpenAI, which was valued at $852 billion in late March. ([Anthropic tops OpenAI as most valuable AI startup, nears...](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-open-ai-startup-value.html)) Both companies are racing to IPO: Anthropic confidentially filed its IPO prospectus with the SEC, and stated it gives the company "the option to go public after the SEC completes its review." ([Anthropic confidentially files IPO prospectus with SEC, p...](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-ipo-s1-prospectus.html))

**So what:**
The "OpenAI is 10x the size" narrative is obsolete. Anthropic's run-rate has surpassed OpenAI's and its valuation now exceeds it. Enterprise procurement teams evaluating primary AI vendors should treat both companies as Tier-1 peers. Anthropic sales should proactively surface these data points — letting buyers assume overwhelming OpenAI dominance is now factually incorrect.

**2. OpenAI removed "safely" from its IRS-filed mission and disbanded its Mission Alignment team; Anthropic's Constitutional AI positioning has never had a clearer lane.**

In nine years of IRS Form 990 filings, OpenAI changed its mission statement six times, finally removing all references to safety in the 2025 form. ([OpenAI changed its mission statement 6 times in 9 years,...](https://fortune.com/2026/02/23/openai-mission-statement-changed-restructuring-forprofit-business/)) While reviewing its latest IRS disclosure form, released in November 2025 and covering 2024, the word "safely" was removed from its mission statement — a change that coincided with its transformation from a nonprofit into a business increasingly focused on profits. ([OpenAI has deleted the word 'safely' from its mission](https://theconversation.com/openai-has-deleted-the-word-safely-from-its-mission-and-its-new-structure-is-a-test-for-whether-ai-serves-society-or-shareholders-274467)) Separately, OpenAI disbanded a team designed to communicate the company's mission to the public and to its own employees; OpenAI confirmed the team's members have been assigned to other roles. The news was first reported by Platformer. ([OpenAI disbands mission alignment team | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/11/openai-disbands-mission-alignment-team-which-focused-on-safe-and-trustworthy-ai-development/)) This follows a prior pattern: OpenAI previously had a "superalignment team" — formed in 2023 and focused on studying long-term existential threats posed by AI — but that team was disbanded in 2024. ([OpenAI disbands mission alignment team | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/11/openai-disbands-mission-alignment-team-which-focused-on-safe-and-trustworthy-ai-development/)) OpenAI states on its website that safety remains core to its work, and the restructured nonprofit OpenAI Foundation retains governance rights over safety practices — the IRS filing language and the marketing page tell different stories.

Anthropic's Constitutional AI approach is architecturally distinct: Anthropic's Constitutional AI research provides an answer by giving language models explicit values determined by a constitution, rather than values determined implicitly via large-scale human feedback — an approach that makes the values of the AI system easier to understand and easier to adjust as needed. ([Claude’s Constitution \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claudes-constitution)) This has grown out of training techniques used since 2023, when Anthropic first began training Claude models using Constitutional AI. The approach has evolved significantly, and the new constitution plays an even more central role in training. ([Claude's new constitution \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-new-constitution))

**So what:**
For regulated buyers in healthcare, finance, legal, and government — who are risk-managing AI vendor decisions — the combination of a governance filing change and the disbanding of two consecutive safety-focused teams is a concrete, documentable differentiator Anthropic can own. Use the IRS filing as the source, not a marketing comparison.

**3. Claude Code owns over half the enterprise coding market today, but Codex has now passed 5 million weekly users and is closing fast.**

Claude Code is estimated to hold approximately 54% of the enterprise AI coding market, according to data from Menlo Ventures' "State of Generative AI Report" — more than double OpenAI's 21% share in the same category. ([Anthropic and Democratization of Technology: How Claude I...](https://www.investing.com/analysis/anthropic-and-democratization-of-technology-how-claude-is-rewriting-the-rules-200681197)) This is a modeled analyst estimate from Menlo Ventures, not audited fact. Claude Code became generally available in May 2025, hit $1B in annualized revenue by November 2025, and reached $2.5B in annualized revenue in February 2026. ([Anthropic revenue, valuation & funding | Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/anthropic/)) OpenAI's Codex is growing rapidly in response: Codex now has more than 5 million weekly active users, up more than six times since OpenAI launched the desktop app in February. ([OpenAI's Codex is the new office productivity booster](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/02/openai-codex-knowledge-workers)) Anthropic released Claude Code as a command-line tool in February 2025; Anthropic was first with a consumer-facing web release in October 2025, and both released native desktop agent apps in 2026. ([OpenAI's Codex is the new office productivity booster](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/02/openai-codex-knowledge-workers))

**So what:**
Anthropic should move aggressively to deepen enterprise lock-in through Claude Code before Codex narrows the gap further. The coding market lead is real today but time-limited. The battlecard advantage must be activated now with active accounts, not preserved for future quarters.

**4. OpenAI's API prices have doubled twice in nine months — a structural commercial opening for Anthropic.**

GPT-5.5 launched with a 2x price increase over GPT-5.4: input tokens increased from $2.50/M to $5.00/M and output tokens from $15/M to $30/M. ([GPT-5.5 Price Increase: What It Actually Costs | OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/announcements/gpt55-cost-analysis)) GPT-5 was released on August 7, 2025, with pricing starting at $1.25 per million input tokens and $10.00 per million output tokens ([GPT 5 API Pricing 2026 - Costs, Performance & Providers](https://pricepertoken.com/pricing-page/model/openai-gpt-5)) — meaning the flagship API price has quadrupled on input tokens in nine months. By contrast, Anthropic's current flagship Claude Opus 4.8 is priced at $5.00/$25.00 per million tokens, with Claude Code generating $2.5B in annualized revenue by February 2026. ([Anthropic revenue, valuation & funding | Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/anthropic/)) Both providers offer 50% batch discounts and ~90% prompt-caching discounts.

**So what:**
Enterprise buyers running high-volume workloads are experiencing OpenAI cost shock in real time. Anthropic's pricing stability — particularly on caching and batch workloads — is a credible commercial differentiator. Lead with TCO math in enterprise deals.

**5. OpenAI wins on consumer reach and multimodal ecosystem; Anthropic should not compete here.**

ChatGPT reached 900 million or more weekly active users by March 2026; OpenAI also reported more than 50 million consumer subscribers and over 1 million business customers. ([OpenAI Statistics 2026: Users, Revenue & Market Share](https://www.getpanto.ai/blog/openai-statistics)) By contrast, in February 2026, Claude recorded 287.93 million web visits, compared with 5.19 billion for ChatGPT, with third-party estimates placing Claude at roughly 4.5% of the global AI chatbot market. ([Anthropic AI Statistics 2026: Users, Revenue & Market Share](https://www.getpanto.ai/blog/anthropic-ai-statistics)) OpenAI's multimodal breadth — image generation, voice, computer use, GPTs store — has no equivalent at Anthropic.

**So what:**
Anthropic should not compete on consumer reach or feature breadth. The winning frame is specialization: enterprise coding, long-context document work, regulated industries, and safety-critical applications. Avoid head-to-head consumer comparisons on features Anthropic does not have.

## Positioning and Differentiation

OpenAI's self-positioning centers on **scale, speed, and infrastructure as mission**. From its funding announcement: OpenAI positions "durable access to compute" as the strategic moat that "advances research, improves products, expands access, and structurally lowers the cost of delivery at scale." ([OpenAI drops ‘safely’ from mission statement after for-pr...](https://www.dagens.com/technology/ai/openai-drops-safely-from-mission-statement-after-for-profit-restructuring)) OpenAI maintains safety-oriented language on its website, stating that advancing AI's "capability, safety, and positive impact" remains central — the IRS filing and the website tell different stories.

Anthropic positions on **safety by design, long-context reasoning, and developer trust**. Constitutional AI gives language models explicit values determined by a constitution, rather than values determined implicitly via large-scale human feedback, making the values of the AI system easier to understand and easier to adjust. ([Claude’s Constitution \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claudes-constitution)) This approach has been core to Claude's training since 2023 and plays an even more central role in Claude's new 2026 constitution. ([Claude's new constitution \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-new-constitution))

## Competitive Battlecard

### Where Anthropic WINS Against OpenAI

- **Enterprise agentic coding.** Claude Code is estimated to hold approximately 54% of the enterprise AI coding market versus OpenAI's 21%, per Menlo Ventures. ([Anthropic and Democratization of Technology: How Claude I...](https://www.investing.com/analysis/anthropic-and-democratization-of-technology-how-claude-is-rewriting-the-rules-200681197)) (Analyst estimate — not audited.) Claude Code reads a codebase, plans a sequence of actions, executes them using real development tools, evaluates the result, and adjusts its approach ([Anthropic says it hit a $30 billion revenue run rate afte...](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-says-it-hit-a-30-billion-revenue-run-rate-after-crazy-80x-growth)) — terminal-native, not a chat interface. Enterprise use represents over half of Claude Code revenue, with customers including Netflix, Spotify, KPMG, L'Oréal, and Salesforce. ([Anthropic revenue, valuation & funding | Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/anthropic/))
- **Long-context document work.** Claude's extended-context models (Opus 4.8, Sonnet 4.6) support up to 1M-token context at standard API pricing with no surcharge — relevant for large codebases, legal documents, and research workflows.
- **Safety governance for regulated industries.** OpenAI changed its mission statement six times across IRS filings, finally removing all references to safety in the 2025 form. ([OpenAI changed its mission statement 6 times in 9 years,...](https://fortune.com/2026/02/23/openai-mission-statement-changed-restructuring-forprofit-business/)) OpenAI disbanded its Mission Alignment team in February 2026 — its second safety-focused team dissolution in two years, following the superalignment team's disbanding in 2024. ([OpenAI disbands mission alignment team | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/11/openai-disbands-mission-alignment-team-which-focused-on-safe-and-trustworthy-ai-development/)) Anthropic's Constitutional AI approach is an auditable, architecturally embedded training methodology, not a post-hoc content filter.
- **API pricing stability.** Anthropic held prices flat across model upgrades in 2026 while OpenAI doubled API prices twice in nine months. For enterprises running large-volume workloads, this predictability is a real procurement advantage.
- **Instruction-following fidelity.** Claude Code posts a 91% customer satisfaction score and a Net Promoter Score of 54 — the highest loyalty metrics in the AI coding tools category, per JetBrains' January 2026 survey of 10,000+ developers. ([AI Coding Market Share 2026: Who's Winning?](https://www.neura.market/directories/cursor/blog/devto-3493354)) (Tier 2E/3S — survey data, not audited.)

**Usable soundbite:**
*"Claude Code holds 54% of the enterprise coding market because it actually runs in your terminal, reads your real codebase, and follows your system prompt. OpenAI's Codex is closing in — but Claude got there first, the gap is measurable, and the enterprises already running it aren't switching."*

### Where It Is a FIGHT

- **Enterprise agentic coding — Codex vs. Claude Code.** Codex now has more than 5 million weekly active users, up more than six times since the desktop app launched in February. ([OpenAI's Codex is the new office productivity booster](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/02/openai-codex-knowledge-workers)) This category could flip within 6–12 months.
- **Enterprise mid-market (150+ seat deals).** Both companies have comparable enterprise compliance stacks (SOC 2, HIPAA BAA, GDPR). Deal outcomes here likely hinge on relationship and pricing, not capability gaps.
- **Writing quality and general reasoning.** Claude has earned a strong developer reputation for writing quality and instruction-following. ChatGPT maintains advantages in speed and ecosystem breadth. Perception shifts with every model update — neither has a durable lock.

**What tips it:**
Claude wins when the buyer has a large codebase, complex document workflows, compliance requirements, or a development team that self-selected Claude. ChatGPT wins when the buyer prioritizes ecosystem breadth, wants image generation, or is already embedded in Microsoft 365. In contested deals, TCO math on API pricing and caching is often decisive — and Anthropic's stable rate card is a current advantage.

**Usable soundbite:**
*"At the flagship tier both models are within noise on most benchmarks. The decision comes down to workflow: if you need an agent that runs in your terminal, manages your actual files, and won't surprise your team with a personality change after a quiet model update — Claude Code is the more mature product today."*

### Where OpenAI BEATS Anthropic

- **Consumer reach and brand recognition.** ChatGPT reached 900 million or more weekly active users by March 2026, with more than 50 million consumer subscribers and over 1 million business customers. ([OpenAI Statistics 2026: Users, Revenue & Market Share](https://www.getpanto.ai/blog/openai-statistics)) Anthropic has a fraction of this consumer mindshare.
- **Multimodal output ecosystem.** OpenAI offers native image generation, voice mode, web browsing, computer use, and a GPTs store with thousands of community-built assistants. Claude generates no images natively and has no equivalent marketplace.
- **Computer use (desktop automation).** Computer use is a category where OpenAI has a measurable lead on OSWorld benchmarks. Claude has no equivalent publicly documented benchmark advantage here.
- **Microsoft distribution channel.** ChatGPT is embedded in Microsoft 365 Copilot, giving OpenAI passive distribution into millions of enterprise seats. Under the restructuring deal, Microsoft retained access to OpenAI's technology through 2032. ## Objection Handling

**Objection 1: "ChatGPT is the market standard — our employees already use it and our Microsoft integration is built around it."**

Acknowledge the real lock-in, then shift the frame. The Microsoft/ChatGPT channel primarily distributes a general-purpose chat interface. For production workloads — especially software development, document processing, and agentic automation — enterprise teams are increasingly buying Claude separately. Claude Code is estimated to hold 54% of the enterprise coding market versus OpenAI's 21%, per Menlo Ventures. ([Anthropic and Democratization of Technology: How Claude I...](https://www.investing.com/analysis/anthropic-and-democratization-of-technology-how-claude-is-rewriting-the-rules-200681197)) (Analyst estimate.) The question is not which tool employees use for quick questions — it is which model runs your production workflows and your agentic pipelines.

**Objection 2: "OpenAI's models are better — GPT-5.5 beats Claude on most benchmarks."**

Don't concede the whole claim. At the flagship tier the models are within close range of each other on leading coding benchmarks, with results varying by test harness and scaffolding — different scaffolding can swing scores by several percentage points, so treat comparisons as directional. The real question is: which model follows your system prompt reliably, maintains a consistent mental model of a large codebase across a long session, and doesn't surprise your engineering team with personality changes after a quiet model update? Claude Code posts a 91% customer satisfaction score and NPS of 54 — the highest loyalty metrics in the AI coding tools category per JetBrains' January 2026 survey. ([AI Coding Market Share 2026: Who's Winning?](https://www.neura.market/directories/cursor/blog/devto-3493354)) OpenAI has documented and acknowledged sycophancy and instruction-following issues in recent months.

**Objection 3: "OpenAI is better-funded and has the compute to stay ahead — why bet on Anthropic?"**

The "bet on the winner" frame has inverted. Anthropic closed a $65 billion Series H at a $965 billion valuation, putting it above rival OpenAI, which was valued at $852 billion in late March. ([Anthropic tops OpenAI as most valuable AI startup, nears...](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-open-ai-startup-value.html)) Amazon has committed up to $25 billion in Anthropic with 5 gigawatts of compute capacity; Anthropic also secured 5 gigawatts via a separate deal with Google and Broadcom. ([Anthropic says it hit a $30 billion revenue run rate afte...](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-says-it-hit-a-30-billion-revenue-run-rate-after-crazy-80x-growth)) Anthropic struck a deal with SpaceX for the full compute capacity at the Colossus 1 data center, paying $1.25 billion per month through May 2029, per SpaceX's IPO prospectus. ([Anthropic set to hit $10.9 billion in revenue during seco...](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-revenue-explosive-growth-ipo-profitable-quarter.html)) More importantly: OpenAI is heading into a pre-IPO period that creates shareholder pressure to raise prices, reduce safety friction, and accelerate revenue. OpenAI changed its mission statement six times across IRS filings, finally removing all references to safety in the 2025 form. ([OpenAI changed its mission statement 6 times in 9 years,...](https://fortune.com/2026/02/23/openai-mission-statement-changed-restructuring-forprofit-business/)) OpenAI does not plan to become cash-flow positive until 2030, while Anthropic is targeting break-even as early as 2028. ([OpenAI adds $111 billion to its cash burn forecast as AI...](https://the-decoder.com/openai-adds-111-billion-to-its-cash-burn-forecast-as-ai-costs-spiral-beyond-projections/)) (Both are modeled projections, not filed targets.) That structural difference matters for enterprise buyers who need to model multi-year vendor stability.
