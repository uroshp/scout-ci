# Competitive Intelligence Brief: Slack vs. Microsoft Teams

## Executive Summary

**1. The EU just broke the structural lock that drove Teams' mass adoption — and Salesforce is now automatically provisioning Slack for all new Salesforce customers.**

On 12 September 2025, the European Commission accepted legally binding commitments from Microsoft concerning Teams, settling an antitrust case ongoing since 2023. ([Microsoft’s commitments on its Teams platform accepted by...](https://www.loyensloeff.com/insights/news--events/news/microsofts-commitments-on-its-teams-platform-accepted-by-european-commission/)) Microsoft will expand the price gap between suites with Teams and those without by 50%, ranging from €1 to €8 depending on the package — commitments that remain in effect for at least seven years. ([Microsoft agrees to unbundle Teams from Office in landmar...](https://www.techspot.com/news/109450-microsoft-reaches-deal-eu-split-teams-office-365.html)) Simultaneously, Salesforce announced a structural counter-move: beginning this summer, Slack will be automatically provisioned, connected, and ready for all new Salesforce customers — AI-powered and Salesforce-aware from day one. ([Slackbot: The New Interface for the Agentic Enterprise. |...](https://www.salesforce.com/slack/slackbot/agent-orchestration/)) These two events, taken together, represent the biggest structural shift in this competitive market in a decade.

**So what:**
In Europe, the "Teams is free" objection has materially weakened — buyers now have a legally enforced right to a lower-price M365 without Teams and a clear fair-comparison moment. Globally, any new Salesforce customer is now a warm Slack lead by default. Prioritize both segments immediately: European enterprise renewals and any account closing or renewing Salesforce contracts.

**2. Microsoft Copilot's AI moat is real and accelerating — but its cost and governance risk create a durable opening.**

Satya Nadella told investors on Microsoft's FY26 Q3 earnings call that Microsoft 365 Copilot now has 20 million paid enterprise seats, with the number of customers deploying more than 50,000 seats having quadrupled. ([Microsoft Copilot Reaches 20 Million Paid Enterprise Seat...](https://letsdatascience.com/news/microsoft-copilot-reaches-20-million-paid-enterprise-seats-eed5aec2)) The product is real and growing. The cost, however, is punishing at scale: an enterprise on E3 moving to Copilot is not paying $30 — they are paying $39 plus $30, for a total of $69 per user per month starting July 2026; for a 5,000-user organization, that is $4.14 million a year. ([Microsoft Copilot M365: What the $30 Per User Price Actua...](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/microsoft-copilot-m365-what-30-per-user-price-actually-iqe1f)) On governance risk: EchoLeak (CVE-2025-32711) is a critical vulnerability in Microsoft 365 Copilot that allows an attacker to steal sensitive organizational data without any user interaction, simply by sending a carefully crafted email. It was characterized as the first "zero-click" exploit against an AI agent. ([EchoLeak (CVE-2025-32711) Show us That AI Security is Cha...](https://checkmarx.com/zero-post/echoleak-cve-2025-32711-show-us-that-ai-security-is-challenging/)) Microsoft patched the specific vulnerability server-side and confirmed no exploitation in the wild. But EchoLeak's significance extends beyond the specific CVE: it is the first documented case of prompt injection being weaponized for concrete data exfiltration in a production AI system, and it reveals a structural attack surface that applies to any LLM-based assistant with access to multiple internal data sources. ([EchoLeak (CVE-2025-32711): What the Microsoft Copilot Pro...](https://sentra.io/blog/copilot-echoleak-prompt-injection))

**So what:**
Slack AI is included in Business+ at $15/user — the all-in price — versus $69+/user for E3+Copilot at enterprise scale from July 2026. Build a cost comparison slide with those exact numbers and use it in every Copilot objection conversation. Pair with the EchoLeak governance point for security-sensitive CIOs: the patch is closed, but the structural risk of a RAG-based AI with broad data access isn't.

**3. Salesforce is transforming Slack into the operating system for Salesforce customers — a position Teams structurally cannot replicate.**

At a small gathering in San Francisco, CEO Marc Benioff and his team unveiled an updated version of Slack with a plethora of new AI features. The most significant is a serious glow-up for Slackbot. The 30 new features, which will be available in the coming months, follow a January 2026 update that gave Slackbot agentic capabilities. ([Salesforce announces an AI-heavy makeover for Slack, with...](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/31/salesforce-announces-an-ai-heavy-makeover-for-slack-with-30-new-features/)) Intelligent AI agents and CRM data from Salesforce are now embedded directly into Slack conversations, starting with Sales, IT, HR Service, and Tableau. Agentforce Sales in Slack brings people, data, and workflows together in one conversational workspace, enabling sales teams to collaborate, surface AI-driven insights, and take action directly alongside live CRM customer data. ([Native AI in Slack: Powering Collaboration and Productivi...](https://www.salesforce.com/slack/native-ai/)) Salesforce's own Tier 1B claim frames it directly: at Dreamforce 2025, Salesforce announced Agentforce 360, the platform that enables every business to become an agentic enterprise, with Slack positioned as the agentic OS for your enterprise. ([Native AI in Slack: Powering Collaboration and Productivi...](https://www.salesforce.com/slack/native-ai/))

**So what:**
For any Salesforce account on Teams, the strategic argument is simple and durable: Teams will always be a release cycle behind on Salesforce AI capabilities. Agentforce agents for sales, IT, HR, and Tableau are Slack-native — Teams gets functional integrations at best. Make this the anchor argument for every Salesforce-heavy account, not a footnote.

**4. Slack's forced AI upgrade is creating real renewal friction — get ahead of it or lose accounts to Teams at renewal.**

The Slack AI add-on is no longer available to purchase on the Slack website. Customers who purchased the add-on continued to pay for it and have access to its features until their first renewal after August 17, 2025. ([Updates to feature availability and pricing for Slack pla...](https://slack.com/help/articles/39264531104275-Updates-to-feature-availability-and-pricing-for-Slack-plans)) Business+ plan pricing increased to $15 from $12.50 per user per month to reflect the significant value added through new advanced AI capabilities and premium Salesforce integrations. ([Salesforce updates Slack pricing | Slack](https://slack.com/blog/news/june-2025-pricing-and-packaging-announcement)) Customers on Pro who relied on the AI add-on face a forced upgrade to Business+ or loss of advanced AI features at renewal — a price step that multiple review platforms flag as a 107% increase from the Pro baseline. Several G2 users mention considering Microsoft Teams as a cost-saving alternative, especially since Teams is bundled with Office 365. ([Slack Pricing 2026](https://www.g2.com/products/slack/pricing))

**So what:**
Renewal friction is real and Teams sales teams will exploit it. Slack CS must proactively engage accounts 90 days before renewal with a concrete value narrative: Business+ is not a price hike, it is Agentforce-native AI, Salesforce CRM integration, compliance exports, and SSO — capabilities that Teams cannot replicate for a Salesforce shop at any price. Every silent renewal is a risk.

**5. Microsoft Copilot's real adoption rate is 4.4% of the M365 base — even after two years and 20 million paid seats — creating a credible counter-narrative on AI maturity.**

Microsoft 365 Copilot reached 20 million paid seats, the company reported during its fiscal Q3 FY2026 earnings call on April 29. Microsoft had reported 15 million paid Copilot seats during its fiscal Q2 2026 earnings call on January 28, 2026 — a 33% increase in paid Copilot seats. ([Microsoft 365 Copilot hits 20 million paid seats](https://www.nojitter.com/ai-automation/microsoft-365-copilot-hits-20-million-paid-seats)) Paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats are likely between 4.2% and 4.4% of total paid Microsoft 365 commercial seats as of fiscal Q3 2026. ([Microsoft 365 Copilot hits 20 million paid seats](https://www.nojitter.com/ai-automation/microsoft-365-copilot-hits-20-million-paid-seats)) Adoption among organizations is also uneven: the single most expensive mistake in Copilot licensing is deploying to all users on day one — enterprise organizations that purchase Copilot licenses for their entire workforce typically see 30–40% of licenses unused within the first 90 days. ([Microsoft 365 Copilot Pricing & Licensing: Enterprise Gui...](https://www.epcgroup.net/microsoft-365-copilot-pricing-licensing-enterprise-guide-2026))

**So what:**
When a prospect says "we're going all-in on Copilot," the honest response is that only 4.4% of Microsoft's own M365 base has done so after two years — and 30–40% of those licenses go unused. Copilot is a compelling roadmap bet; Slack AI is a working product included today. Lead on use-case specificity and current delivery, not future vision.

## Recent Strategic Moves

**EU unbundling — September 2025 (highest signal):**

The European Union accepted commitments from Microsoft to unbundle its Teams workplace communication platform from its popular productivity apps. The decision essentially exempts Microsoft from receiving a potentially hefty antitrust fine after the company was accused by the European Commission of breaching competition rules with the "abusive" bundling of its Teams and Office products. ([Microsoft sidesteps hefty EU fine with Teams unbundling deal](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/09/12/microsoft-avoids-big-fine-as-eu-accepts-deal-to-unbundle-teams.html)) Microsoft expanded the price gap between suites with Teams and those without by 50%, ranging from €1 to €8 depending on the package. The changes remain in effect for at least seven years. ([Microsoft agrees to unbundle Teams from Office in landmar...](https://www.techspot.com/news/109450-microsoft-reaches-deal-eu-split-teams-office-365.html)) Microsoft also agreed to publish documentation supporting greater interoperability between Teams rivals and Office products — a requirement lasting a decade — and European customers will be able to export Teams messaging data to competing platforms. ([Microsoft agrees to unbundle Teams from Office in landmar...](https://www.techspot.com/news/109450-microsoft-reaches-deal-eu-split-teams-office-365.html))

**Signal:** The bundling advantage that drove Teams' mass adoption is now legally constrained in Europe for seven years, with interoperability requirements running a full decade. This is the single most important regulatory event in this competitive space in a decade.

**Microsoft 365 price increases effective July 1, 2026 (pricing signal):**

Microsoft announced in December 2025 that M365 base licenses are increasing effective July 1, 2026: E3 moves to $39 and E5 moves to $60 per user per month. ([Microsoft Copilot M365: What the $30 Per User Price Actua...](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/microsoft-copilot-m365-what-30-per-user-price-actually-iqe1f)) Business Standard rises to $14/user/month from July 2026 (recently increased from $12.50). ([How Much Does Microsoft Copilot Cost Per User in 2026?](https://copilot-experts.com/microsoft-copilot-cost-per-user/))

**Signal:** Microsoft is raising prices across the entire M365 stack. Every account renewing post-July 2026 faces a higher total cost of ownership for Teams + Copilot than their current contract reflects. This is live negotiation leverage for Slack now.

**Copilot AI agents in Teams (September–November 2025 — product signal):**

Per Microsoft's own Tier 1B announcement: in May 2025, Microsoft launched new AI agent discovery on mobile, updated the Teams AI Library, and introduced the Agent2Agent Protocol (A2A), allowing agents to communicate peer-to-peer without centralized intermediaries. ([Microsoft Teams Statistics 2026: How Teams Dominates Coll...](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/microsoft-teams-statistics/)) At Ignite 2025, Microsoft announced the Facilitator agent (which drives meeting agendas, takes notes, and manages actions) as generally available, and expanded agents in Teams Channels to work with third-party apps via MCP servers.

**Signal:** Microsoft is building Copilot from individual productivity tool into team-level AI collaboration — a direct competitive response to Slack's Agentforce strategy. Do not underestimate this roadmap.

**Salesforce auto-provisioning Slack for all new Salesforce customers (announced 2026 — Slack-side signal):**

Beginning this summer, Slack will be automatically provisioned, connected, and ready for all new Salesforce customers — AI-powered and Salesforce-aware from day one. ([Slackbot: The New Interface for the Agentic Enterprise. |...](https://www.salesforce.com/slack/slackbot/agent-orchestration/)) AI-enabled apps built for Slack have already grown 690% year over year. ([Slackbot: The New Interface for the Agentic Enterprise. |...](https://www.salesforce.com/slack/slackbot/agent-orchestration/))

**Signal:** Salesforce is systematically making Slack the default enterprise AI interface for its install base. Every new Salesforce sale is now also a Slack distribution event.

**Slackbot 30-feature refresh — March 31, 2026 (product signal):**

On March 31, 2026, Salesforce announced more than 30 new AI-powered capabilities for Slackbot, repositioning Slack and Slackbot specifically as the central nervous system of the modern enterprise. ([Slackbot Just Got 30 New AI Features - Salesforce Break](https://salesforcebreak.com/2026/04/06/slackbot-30-new-ai-features/)) Slackbot functions as a Model Context Protocol (MCP) client, routing tasks to Agentforce and thousands of connected apps. ## Positioning and Differentiation

**How Teams positions itself:**

Teams positions primarily on **ecosystem unity** — the argument that for organizations already on Microsoft 365, Teams is the zero-friction path to collaboration. Microsoft's own Tier 1B claim: the company states that Teams serves 320 million monthly active users across 181 markets and frames the future of work as "Frontier Firms — organizations that are human-led and agent-operated," with Teams as the workspace where this happens.

For enterprise and regulated industries, Teams' secondary positioning centers on **compliance and governance**. Microsoft's own claim: organizations are no longer locked into a single collaboration platform — customers who prefer alternatives to Teams can now integrate Microsoft 365 more seamlessly, ensuring their data remains accessible and workflows stay connected across different tools. ([Microsoft Teams Unbundled | SoftwareOne blog](https://www.softwareone.com/en/blog/articles/2025/09/18/microsoft-unbundles-teams))

**Real differentiators (evidence-based):**

- **Microsoft 365 native integration:** Teams integrates seamlessly with Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and OneDrive — users can edit documents directly within Teams without switching applications.
- **Video conferencing at scale:** Teams supports large-scale events — up to 50,000-attendee town halls. Slack Huddles top out at 50 participants and cannot be recorded for absentees.
- **Teams Phone:** Teams' 80 million Phone users, of whom over 20 million use PSTN-based calling, further illustrate the platform's expansion beyond its original video and chat roots. ([Microsoft Teams Statistics 2026: Chat Volume, Meeting Hou...](https://speakwiseapp.com/blog/microsoft-teams-statistics)) This is an area Teams owns outright.
- **Regulated vertical dominance:** Teams has become the default collaboration platform in sectors like education, government, and healthcare — verticals where Microsoft's compliance certifications (Purview, Defender, Entra) matter most.

## Competitive Battlecard

### WHERE SLACK WINS

**Salesforce/CRM-integrated enterprise buyers:**

For any organization running Salesforce, Slack is the unambiguous winner. Intelligent AI agents and CRM data from Salesforce are now embedded directly into Slack conversations, starting with Sales, IT, HR Service, and Tableau. Agentforce Sales in Slack brings people, data, and workflows together in one conversational workspace, enabling sales teams to collaborate, surface AI-driven insights, and take action directly alongside live CRM customer data. ([Native AI in Slack: Powering Collaboration and Productivi...](https://www.salesforce.com/slack/native-ai/)) Teams' Salesforce integration exists but is not Salesforce's strategic R&D priority. The Slackbot reimagining, Agentforce-native experiences, and MCP server connectivity are all Slack-first.

**Why they win:** Slack can surface, update, and act on Salesforce data — leads, cases, opportunities — without leaving the channel. Teams cannot. The workflow isn't just tighter; it is structurally different.

**Implication:** Any Salesforce customer on Teams is accepting a second-class Salesforce experience for the foreseeable future.

*"If you're running Salesforce, Teams will always be one release cycle behind on what Salesforce actually builds. Slack is the front door to Agentforce — Teams is a side entrance."*

---

**User experience and adoption speed:**

G2 reviewers note that Slack's ease of use is a significant advantage, with many praising its intuitive interface and quick implementation process. New users often find it straightforward to get started, which is a crucial factor for teams looking to adopt new software quickly. ([Compare Microsoft Teams vs. Slack | G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/microsoft-teams-vs-slack)) Slack supports over 2,600 third-party integrations in its app directory ([Slack vs. Microsoft Teams Statistics 2026: Who Leads Team...](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/slack-vs-microsoft-teams-statistics/)) — a breadth Teams does not match for heterogeneous stacks.

**Why they win:** Faster adoption, lower training cost, and higher voluntary daily engagement. Users choose Slack; users tolerate Teams.

*"Slack adoption is pull — people request it. Teams adoption is push — IT deploys it. Which one actually gets used?"*

### WHERE IT IS A FIGHT

**Mid-market enterprise accounts not on Salesforce:**

Buyers with 200–2,000 employees already on Microsoft 365 are genuinely contested. For an organization already paying $7–14.50/user/month for M365, Teams is marginal cost; Slack requires a new budget line. Slack's counter is UX quality, integration breadth, and Agentforce AI — but the switching cost argument is real.

**What tips it toward Slack:** If the organization has meaningful tool diversity outside Microsoft (Google Workspace, AWS, Salesforce, non-Microsoft project management), Slack's tool-agnostic architecture wins. If your organization uses Slack, Jira, Confluence, GitHub, Salesforce, Notion, or Google Drive, Copilot either cannot access that data or requires custom connectors to bridge the gap. ([Microsoft Copilot Pricing: What It Really Costs in 2026 |...](https://www.gosearch.ai/blog/microsoft-copilot-pricing/))

**What tips it toward Teams:** Tight IT budgets, a Microsoft EA already in place, or an IT team that prefers single-vendor governance.

*"The honest answer is: if your whole company runs on Outlook, SharePoint, and Azure — Teams probably makes financial sense. If you have a mix of tools and a team that actually has to want to use the platform, the math changes."*

Both platforms have AI agents embedded in channels, meeting summaries, and workflow automation. AI-enabled apps built for Slack have already grown 690% year over year. ([Slackbot: The New Interface for the Agentic Enterprise. |...](https://www.salesforce.com/slack/slackbot/agent-orchestration/)) Microsoft's Copilot Tuning (announced at Build 2025) allows organizations to fine-tune AI models on proprietary data — a compelling enterprise differentiator.

**What tips it toward Slack:** Price. Slack AI is included in Business+ ($15/user). An enterprise on E3 moving to Copilot is not paying $30 — they are paying $39 plus $30, for a total of $69 per user per month starting July 2026. For a 5,000-user organization, that is $4.14 million a year. ([Microsoft Copilot M365: What the $30 Per User Price Actua...](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/microsoft-copilot-m365-what-30-per-user-price-actually-iqe1f))

**What tips it toward Teams:** Depth of integration across the full Microsoft 365 data graph — Copilot can reason across emails, calendar, SharePoint, and Teams simultaneously in a way Slack AI cannot without connectors. And Copilot's 20M paid seats signals real enterprise commitment, not vaporware.

*"Teams AI knows your emails, your calendar, your SharePoint — everything Microsoft. Slack AI knows your Salesforce data, your channels, and your conversations. Which context matters more for your workflows?"*

## Objection Handling

**Objection 1: "Teams is free for us — we already pay for Microsoft 365."**

This is the most common and most legitimate Teams objection. Do not pretend it isn't true.

**Response:** For organizations that are 80%+ Microsoft-native with no Salesforce footprint and no mixed tool stack, Teams may genuinely be the right financial call — acknowledge this honestly. But reframe the comparison from license cost to total value and adoption. Ask: what is the cost of a tool your team tolerates versus one they actually want to use? G2 reviewers note that Slack's ease of use is a significant advantage, with many praising its intuitive interface and quick implementation process — new users find it straightforward to get started. ([Compare Microsoft Teams vs. Slack | G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/microsoft-teams-vs-slack)) Then anchor on the Salesforce/AI workflow argument: if the organization uses Salesforce, intelligent AI agents and CRM data from Salesforce are now embedded directly into Slack conversations, starting with Sales, IT, HR Service, and Tableau ([Native AI in Slack: Powering Collaboration and Productivi...](https://www.salesforce.com/slack/native-ai/)) — Teams gets functional parity on the basics, but the agentic future Salesforce is building is a Slack story.
